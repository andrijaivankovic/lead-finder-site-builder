      Connect your mailbox

      The Inbox reads and sends through your own mailbox, the one on your own domain. It needs three things:

      1. A mailbox that allows IMAP and SMTP. On Zoho that is Mail Lite or above; the free plan has neither.
      2. Its address and provider under Mailbox on the Settings page.
      3. An app password from the mailbox, put into .env after MAILBOX_PASSWORD=.
